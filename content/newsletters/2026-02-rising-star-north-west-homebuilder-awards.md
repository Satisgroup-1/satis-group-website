Shiro Rauniar has been named Rising Star of the Year at the North West Homebuilder Awards.

Shiro co-founded Satis Group and leads funding strategy, investor relations and risk management across the business, working on residential development, conversions, new-build schemes and high-spec retrofit.

## An award for the people, not the buildings

Most of what a developer gets judged on is the finished product. This one is about the person behind it, which makes a change worth marking: putting schemes together, finding the funding, and building the team that delivers them is the part nobody photographs.

Congratulations to Shiro from everyone at Satis Group, and to the other finalists in the category.

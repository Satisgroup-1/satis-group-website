Shaunak Rauniar has won Rising Star of the Year at the Property Week RESI Awards 2025 for his early impact and his SME network leadership in UK residential development. He collected the award for The Developers Club.

## Why the category matters

The RESI Awards judge the residential sector nationally, and Rising Star looks past finished schemes to the people shaping how the sector works. SME developers deliver a large share of the country's new homes while carrying the least support, so leadership that connects them up is worth as much as any single building.

Congratulations to Shaunak, and to everyone shortlisted alongside him.

Satis Group appears in Insider's Property Disruptors feature, Building the Future.

The series looks at the businesses changing how property gets delivered across the region. We were asked about our approach to redevelopment, and gave the same answer we always give: the buildings worth doing are the overlooked ones, and the way to do them well is to keep the whole process in house.

## What we told them

- Every project is run in house, from acquisition and planning through construction to sale.
- We look for good bones in the wrong condition rather than easy sites.
- Manchester and the wider North West have the demand and the stock to keep that model working.

Our thanks to the Insider team for including us.

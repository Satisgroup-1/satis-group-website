Refurbishment at Barrington House is complete and the first residents move in this month. The Victorian building sits in the quietest pocket of Altrincham, ten minutes' walk from the Metrolink, and the finished interiors pair its original brickwork with a palette of brushed bronze, smoked oak and natural stone.

## Three ways to live

- En-suite rooms, furnished with bills included
- A one-bedroom penthouse across a two-storey mezzanine
- A two-bedroom basement flat with full-height windows

Lettings are live through SpareRoom and Rightmove, with viewings by appointment. The development page on this site has the full details.

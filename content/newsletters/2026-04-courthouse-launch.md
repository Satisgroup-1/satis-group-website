The Courthouse, our conversion of the former Macclesfield courthouse, has officially launched. The 1934 building has been re-imagined with a new roof-top extension and thirteen one and two-bedroom apartments, each finished with quartz worktops, contemporary branded sanitaryware and a ten-year structural warranty.

## Why Macclesfield

Sitting on the edge of the Peak District, Macclesfield pairs contemporary culture and cuisine with spectacular landscapes, and the station is half a mile from the front door.

Sales are being handled in-house by the Satis Group team, so buyers deal with us directly. Visit the development page on this site for the full specification and apartment schedule.

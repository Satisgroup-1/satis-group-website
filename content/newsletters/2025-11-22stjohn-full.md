title: 22 St John reaches full occupancy
date: 2025-11-06
summary: All thirteen suites at our Grade II listed Manchester workspace are now let, two years after the remodel completed.
---

Every suite at 22 St John is now occupied. The Grade II listed Georgian building between Deansgate and Spinningfields was remodelled to provide thirteen private office suites, and demand has been steady since the doors opened — the final suite was taken last week by a design studio relocating from the Northern Quarter.

## What tenants tell us

The same three things come up again and again: the heritage features, the natural daylight, and the fact that a small team can walk to Spinningfields in three minutes without paying Spinningfields rents.

A waiting list is now open for suites as they become available. Enquiries go through our appointed agent, Sixteen Real Estate.

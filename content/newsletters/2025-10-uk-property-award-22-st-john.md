22 St John has been recognised at the UK Property Awards, taking a five-star award for Best Commercial Renovation & Redevelopment across Greater Manchester and the United Kingdom.

The building sits on the only surviving Georgian terraced street in central Manchester. Restyled rather than stripped out, it retains its heritage while working as thirteen private office suites over three floors, with communal space, meeting and Zoom rooms, and a business lounge.

## Why this one matters to us

Five stars is the top rating the awards give, and the category is renovation rather than new build. That is the whole argument of the business in one line: the best thing to do with a good building is usually to bring it back, not knock it down.

## About the building

- Grade II listed Georgian terrace, Deansgate
- Thirteen private office suites over three floors
- Heritage features retained throughout the remodel
- Fully fitted suites from three desks upwards
